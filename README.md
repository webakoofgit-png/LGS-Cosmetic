---

# PRIMARY DESIGN REFERENCE

Use the overall **design quality, section hierarchy, spacing, product presentation, category browsing experience, promotional layouts and premium cosmetics-shopping feel of RENÉE Cosmetics** as the main UI/UX inspiration:

https://www.reneecosmetics.in/

IMPORTANT:

* I want a similar **premium cosmetics e-commerce visual experience and section flow**.
* Do NOT create a pixel-for-pixel copy.
* Do NOT copy RENÉE branding, text, copyrighted banners, images or assets.
* Recreate the overall design language using Lucky Varieties Beauty Mall's own identity.
* The final website must look like an established premium beauty brand, not a generic local shop template.
* Avoid excessive gradients, glassmorphism and random floating cards.
* Keep the design clean, editorial, luxurious and product-focused.

# FINAL DESIGN REQUIREMENT

The final result must immediately feel like a **serious premium cosmetics shopping website**, comparable in visual polish and merchandising depth to established D2C beauty brands.

Take strong inspiration from the **section flow, product merchandising approach and premium shopping experience of RENÉE Cosmetics**, but give Lucky Varieties Beauty Mall its own visual identity.

The website must balance two audiences:

**B2C:** everyday beauty customers

and

**B2B:** salon/parlour professionals.

The LGS own-brand range must receive dedicated visibility and should feel like a brand within the larger Lucky Varieties Beauty Mall ecosystem.

Most importantly:

**Do not generate a generic template.**

Focus heavily on:

* professional visual hierarchy
* premium beauty imagery
* excellent spacing
* polished product cards
* strong mobile UX
* conversion-focused CTAs
* real-store authenticity
* responsive e-commerce interactions
* elegant animations
* consistent brand identity

Build the entire frontend as a polished, production-ready experience with reusable components and realistic product/category data structures so backend/e-commerce integration can be added later without redesigning the UI.

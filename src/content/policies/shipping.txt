 SHIPPING POLICY

Last Updated: September 7, 2026

Welcome to Lgscosmetics.

We are committed to providing a smooth, reliable, and convenient delivery experience to our customers. This Shipping Policy explains how orders are processed, packed, shipped, tracked, and delivered.

 1. Order Processing

Once your order is successfully placed and payment is confirmed, we begin processing your order.

Before dispatch:

* Products are checked for quality and condition.
* Products are carefully packed to help prevent damage during transportation.
* The order is handed over to our selected delivery/courier partner.
* Customers may receive order confirmation and shipping updates through the contact details provided at the time of purchase.

Orders may take additional time during weekends, public holidays, sale periods, or periods of high order volume.

 2. Shipping Locations

Lucky Varieties Beauty Mall currently provides delivery to serviceable locations within India.

Delivery availability may depend on the customer's PIN code and the service coverage of our courier partners.

If a particular PIN code is not serviceable, we may not be able to deliver the order to that location.

 3. Estimated Delivery Time

We generally aim to dispatch orders within 1–4 business days after successful order confirmation.

The actual delivery time may vary depending on:

* Customer's delivery location
* PIN code
* Courier partner
* Product availability
* Weather conditions
* Public holidays
* High order volumes
* Sale or promotional periods
* Other circumstances beyond our reasonable control

The delivery date shown at checkout or communicated to the customer is an estimated timeframe and not an absolute guarantee.

 4. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if applicable, will be communicated or displayed to the customer before the order is confirmed.

Shipping charges may vary depending on:

* Order value
* Delivery location
* Package size or weight
* Delivery method
* Promotional offers

From time to time, Lucky Varieties Beauty Mall may offer free shipping promotions or special delivery offers.

The applicable shipping charge shown at the time of checkout/order confirmation shall be considered final for that order.

 5. Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, tracking information may be provided through the registered mobile number, WhatsApp, email, or other available communication method.

If a tracking facility is available, customers can use the tracking details provided to check the current status of their shipment.

Tracking information may take some time to become active after the courier partner receives the package.

 6. Multiple Shipments

In some cases, products from the same order may be dispatched separately.

This may happen because:

* Products are available at different locations.
* Products require separate packaging.
* Different products are handled by different delivery partners.

If your order is divided into multiple shipments, you may receive the products at different times.

 7. Delivery Attempt

Our delivery partner will attempt to deliver the order to the address provided by the customer.

Customers should provide:

* Correct name
* Complete delivery address
* Valid PIN code
* Active mobile number

If the delivery partner cannot reach the customer, the order may be re-attempted according to the courier partner's delivery process.

Repeated unsuccessful delivery attempts may result in the package being returned to us.

 8. Incorrect or Incomplete Address

Customers are responsible for providing accurate delivery information.

Lucky Varieties Beauty Mall will not be responsible for delays or failed deliveries caused by:

* Incorrect address
* Incomplete address
* Incorrect PIN code
* Incorrect mobile number
* Customer being unavailable
* Customer refusing delivery
* Other information provided incorrectly by the customer

Additional delivery charges may apply if a shipment needs to be re-dispatched because of incorrect information provided by the customer.

 9. Delayed Delivery

Although we make reasonable efforts to deliver orders within the estimated timeframe, delays may occur due to circumstances beyond our control.

These may include:

* Severe weather
* Natural disasters
* Transportation problems
* Courier delays
* Public holidays
* Government restrictions
* Technical problems
* High order volumes
* Sale events
* Other unforeseen circumstances

In such cases, we will try to assist the customer and provide available shipment information.

 10. Damaged Package

Customers are advised to check the package carefully at the time of delivery.

If the package appears visibly damaged, opened, or tampered with, customers should document the condition of the package and contact Lucky Varieties Beauty Mall as soon as possible.

Where appropriate, we may request photographs, videos, order details, or other information to investigate the issue.

 11. Missing or Incorrect Product

If you receive:

* A missing product
* An incorrect product
* A damaged product
* An incomplete order

please contact us as soon as possible with your order details.

We may request supporting photographs, videos, packaging information, or other relevant details before processing the complaint.

Any replacement, refund, or other resolution will be subject to our applicable Return & Refund Policy.

 12. Cancellation After Dispatch

Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may not always be possible.

Customers wishing to cancel an order should contact us as early as possible before dispatch.

Cancellation requests will be handled according to our applicable Cancellation Policy.

 13. Return to Sender

An order may be returned to Lucky Varieties Beauty Mall if:

* The customer is unavailable after repeated delivery attempts.
* The address is incorrect or incomplete.
* The customer refuses delivery.
* The PIN code is not serviceable.
* The courier partner is unable to complete delivery.
* Other circumstances prevent successful delivery.

If a returned order needs to be shipped again, additional shipping charges may apply where applicable.

 14. Cash on Delivery

If Cash on Delivery (COD) is available, it will be offered only for eligible orders and serviceable locations.

COD availability, minimum/maximum order value, and other applicable conditions may vary.

The payment method available at the time of placing the order shall be considered applicable to that order.

 15. International Shipping

At present, Lucky Varieties Beauty Mall primarily focuses on deliveries within India.

International shipping may not be available unless specifically confirmed by us.

If international shipping is introduced in the future, applicable shipping charges, customs duties, taxes, documentation requirements, and delivery conditions will be communicated separately.

 16. Contact for Shipping Issues

For questions regarding shipping, delivery, tracking, delayed orders, damaged packages, or other delivery-related issues, please contact Lucky Varieties Beauty Mall using the contact information available on our website.

 17. Policy Updates

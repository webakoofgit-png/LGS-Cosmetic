



 CANCELLATION AND RETURN POLICY

Last Updated: September 7, 2026

At Lgscosmetics, customer satisfaction is important to us. We aim to provide quality products and a convenient shopping experience.

This Cancellation and Return Policy explains the conditions applicable to order cancellations, returns, replacements, and refunds for purchases made through our website.

 1. ORDER CANCELLATION

 Cancellation Before Shipment

You may request cancellation of your order before it has been shipped.

To request cancellation, please contact Lucky Varieties Beauty Mall with your:

* Order Number / Order ID
* Customer Name
* Registered Mobile Number
* Reason for cancellation

If the order has not yet been dispatched, we will make reasonable efforts to cancel it.

Once an order has been packed, handed over to the courier partner, or dispatched, cancellation may not always be possible.

 Cancellation After Shipment

If your order has already been dispatched, please contact us for assistance.

Depending on the status of the shipment, you may be required to receive the product and follow the applicable return procedure.

Any refund will be processed only after the returned product is received and inspected, where applicable.

 2. CANCELLATION BY LUCKY VARIETIES BEAUTY MALL

In certain circumstances, we may cancel an order.

This may include:

* Product being out of stock
* Incorrect pricing or product information
* Payment verification failure
* Delivery location being unserviceable
* Suspected fraudulent transaction
* Technical or system errors
* Product being unavailable or discontinued
* Other circumstances preventing us from fulfilling the order

If a prepaid order is cancelled by us, the eligible amount will be refunded according to the original payment method or another mutually agreed method.

 3. RETURN POLICY

Eligible products may be returned within 7 days from the date of delivery, subject to the conditions stated in this policy.

A return request must be raised within the applicable return period.

Customers should provide:

* Order Number / Order ID
* Reason for return
* Photographs or video of the product, where required
* Photographs of the packaging, where applicable
* Invoice or proof of purchase, where required

Submitting a return request does not automatically guarantee approval. Every request may be reviewed before acceptance.

 4. CONDITIONS FOR ACCEPTING RETURNS

To qualify for a return, the product should generally be:

* Unused
* Unopened, where applicable
* In its original condition
* In its original packaging
* With original tags and labels intact
* With seals intact, where applicable
* Accompanied by all accessories and components
* Accompanied by complimentary/free products supplied with the order, where applicable

Products that do not satisfy the applicable return conditions may not be accepted.

 5. NON-RETURNABLE PRODUCTS

Due to hygiene, health, safety, or product-specific reasons, certain products may not be eligible for return or exchange.

Return or exchange may not be accepted if:

* The product has been used.
* The product has been opened where hygiene or safety is involved.
* The original seal has been broken.
* The product has been altered or modified.
* The product has been damaged due to misuse.
* Original packaging, tags, labels, accessories, or components are missing.
* The product is specifically marked as non-returnable.
* The return request is submitted after the applicable return period.

For hygiene reasons, certain beauty, cosmetic, skincare, personal-care, and similar products may be non-returnable once opened or used, unless the product received was damaged, defective, or incorrect and the claim is approved by us.

 6. DAMAGED, DEFECTIVE OR WRONG PRODUCT

If you receive a product that is:

* Damaged
* Defective
* Incorrect
* Different from the product ordered
* Missing an item or component

please contact Lucky Varieties Beauty Mall as soon as possible after delivery.

We recommend taking photographs and an unboxing video when opening the package, especially if there is visible damage to the outer packaging.

We may request photographs, videos, packaging details, invoice details, or other reasonable evidence to verify the issue.

Once the claim is verified and approved, depending on the circumstances and product availability, we may provide:

* Replacement
* Exchange
* Refund
* Another appropriate resolution

 7. REPLACEMENT

Replacement requests are subject to product availability and approval of the return request.

If the same product is unavailable, we may offer an alternative solution or eligible refund.

A replacement will normally be processed after the original product has been received and inspected, unless otherwise agreed.

 8. PARTIAL RETURNS

If your order contains multiple eligible products, you may request a return for an individual product without returning the entire order.

The product being returned must meet all applicable return conditions.

Any accessories, components, promotional items, or complimentary products supplied specifically with the returned product may also need to be returned.

 9. FREE GIFTS & PROMOTIONAL PRODUCTS

If a product was supplied with a free gift or promotional item, the free item may form part of the original purchase.

Where required, the free gift or promotional product must also be returned along with the main product.

Failure to return the applicable complimentary product may affect the refund amount or return eligibility.

 10. RETURN PICKUP

Where reverse pickup service is available, we may arrange collection through our delivery partner.

Reverse pickup availability depends on the customer's location and courier serviceability.

If reverse pickup is unavailable at your location, you may be asked to send the eligible product to the return address communicated by Lucky Varieties Beauty Mall.

Please do not send any product without first contacting us and obtaining confirmation of the return request.

 11. RETURN INSPECTION

All returned products may undergo inspection after being received.

We will check factors such as:

* Product condition
* Original packaging
* Tags and labels
* Seals
* Accessories
* Free gifts
* Reason for return

Refund or replacement will generally be processed only after the return has been verified and approved.

 12. REFUNDS

For approved returns or cancellations, eligible refunds will be initiated after the required verification process is completed.

For prepaid orders, refunds will generally be initiated to the original payment method where technically possible.

For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, eligible refunds may be processed through bank transfer, UPI, or another available refund method after the customer provides the required information.

After we initiate the refund, the time taken for the amount to appear in your account may depend on your bank, payment gateway, UPI provider, or other payment service provider.

 13. SHIPPING CHARGES

Original shipping or delivery charges may not be refundable unless the return is due to an error attributable to Lucky Varieties Beauty Mall, such as an approved claim involving an incorrect, damaged, or defective product.

Return or reverse-shipping charges may apply in other cases and will be communicated where applicable.

 14. SALE & PROMOTIONAL PRODUCTS

Special conditions may apply to products purchased during:

* Sales
* Clearance sales
* Special promotions
* Discount campaigns
* Combo offers
* Limited-time offers

Certain discounted, clearance, promotional, or specially marked products may be non-returnable or subject to different return conditions.

Customers are advised to review any product-specific conditions before completing the purchase.

 15. EXCHANGE

Where exchange is available, it will be subject to:

* Return eligibility
* Product availability
* Product condition
* Price difference, if applicable
* Delivery serviceability

If an exchange cannot be provided, an eligible refund or other appropriate resolution may be offered.

 16. RETURN ABUSE OR FRAUDULENT CLAIMS

Lucky Varieties Beauty Mall reserves the right to reject return, replacement, cancellation, or refund requests where there is reasonable evidence of:

* Fraudulent activity
* Product misuse
* Repeated abuse of the return policy
* False claims
* Product substitution
* Tampering
* Intentional damage

Such decisions will be made after reasonable review of the circumstances.

 17. CONTACT US

For cancellation, return, replacement, or refund assistance, please contact Lucky Varieties Beauty Mall through the contact details provided on our website.

You can also connect with us through our official social-media profiles:

When contacting us regarding an order, please keep your Order ID, registered mobile number, and relevant photographs/videos ready for faster assistance.

 18. POLICY CHANGES

Lucky Varieties Beauty Mall reserves the right to modify or update this Cancellation and Return Policy when necessary.

Any revised policy will become effective when published on our website.

Customers are encouraged to review this policy before making a purchase.
